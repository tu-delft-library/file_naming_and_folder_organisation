# Welcome to the mini-module: "File Naming & Folder Organisation!"

This mini-module is designed for students at TU Delft who are working with files and folders during assignments and projects. It is also designed to teach foundational skills to students in computer programming courses who are learning to summon files through the command line interface. 

Do you have additional feedback or questions? Please contact Data Literacy Project leader Paige Folsom: p.m.folsom@tudelft.nl

You can also suggest edits through Github! Please visit the [Open GitHub repository](https://github.com/tu-delft-library/planning_for_rdm) for this mini-module.
